ABOUT GREENNGAGE

A Greener Campus Starts With You.

GreeNgage is a gamified campus engagement platform designed to encourage students to participate in sustainability initiatives and campus activities.

Through interactive quests, photo submissions, achievements, and rewards, students can turn simple everyday actions into meaningful contributions to a cleaner and more engaging campus environment.

🌎

Every completed quest is another step toward building a more responsible campus community.
